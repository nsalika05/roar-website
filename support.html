Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a bug?

If you discover a bug, email us with as much detail as possible, including your device model, operating system, and the steps that led to the issue.

How do I report incorrect transit information?

Community reports are submitted by riders. If you notice inaccurate information, you can report it directly in the app or contact us by email.

How do I suggest a feature?

We're always looking for ways to improve ROAR. Feature suggestions are welcome through our support email.

Which transit systems does ROAR support?

ROAR is currently focused on the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA). Support for additional transit systems may be added in future updates.
