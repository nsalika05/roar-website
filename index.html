Built for safer transit.

ROAR was created to give riders a simple way to share and view transit incidents in real time. By combining community reports with an interactive map, ROAR helps riders stay informed before and during every trip.

Safety Notice: ROAR helps riders share community-reported transit information, but it is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or witnessing a crime, call 911 or contact local authorities immediately.
