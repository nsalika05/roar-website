Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

ROAR is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices regarding your information.

Information We Collect

When you submit a transit report, the app may collect:

  • Your report details (description, incident type, station, and train line)
  • The location you choose to submit with the report
  • An anonymous Firebase-generated device identifier used to prevent duplicate confirmations and abuse

ROAR does not require users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.

Location Information

ROAR requests access to your location to allow you to submit accurate transit reports and to display nearby incidents on the map.

Location access is optional. You may deny location permission, although some features may not function as intended.

How We Use Information

Collected information is used to:

  • Display user-submitted transit reports
  • Show reports on the interactive map
  • Help prevent spam and duplicate interactions
  • Maintain and improve the reliability of the app

Data Storage

ROAR stores report information using Google Firebase. Data is transmitted securely using industry-standard encryption.

Data Sharing

ROAR does not sell personal information.

Information is not shared with advertisers or marketing partners.

Third-Party Services

ROAR uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (data storage)
  • Google Maps Platform (map display and location services)

These services may process information as necessary to provide their functionality.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Deny location permission through your device settings
  • Stop using the app at any time

Children's Privacy

ROAR is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
